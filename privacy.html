Privacy

Life OS · last updated 9 November 2026

Life OS is a personal dashboard built and used by one person. It is not a commercial service and has no other users.

Google Calendar

  • Life OS asks for read-only access to Google Calendar (calendar.readonly) and your email address.
  • It reads your calendar list and events (4 weeks back and ahead for the daily schedule; about 5 weeks back and 6 months ahead for calendars shown in the Month view), and shows them in Life OS.
  • It never creates, changes or deletes anything in Google Calendar.
  • Events are stored in your own Life OS account (Supabase, EU region London) so every device you sign in on sees them. Row-level security keeps them visible only to you.
  • The Google access token is stored only on the server and is never sent to the browser.
  • Data is not sold, shared, used for ads or used to train AI models.

Removing access

Settings → Calendar → Disconnect revokes access and removes all calendar data from Life OS. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Life OS’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
